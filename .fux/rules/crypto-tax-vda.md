**Rule (external):** Crypto and other Virtual Digital Assets (VDAs) have their
own punitive regime under s.115BBH — none of the equity/debt rules apply:

- **Flat 30%** (plus surcharge/cess) on every transfer gain, regardless of
  holding period or income slab. No long-term concession exists.
- **No deductions** other than cost of acquisition — exchange fees, gas, and
  interest are not deductible.
- **No set-off, no carry-forward**: a VDA loss cannot offset *any* income —
  not even another VDA's gain. Each profitable trade is taxed in full even in
  a net-loss year.
- **1% TDS** (s.194S) is withheld on transfers above modest thresholds — it
  shows up as a cash-flow drag even on break-even trades.

**Source:** Income-tax Act s.115BBH and s.194S (Finance Act 2022). Verify
current TDS thresholds before relying on a number.

**Applies to:** any P&L, projection, or rebalancing advice touching the crypto
sleeve. After-tax expected return on crypto is materially below the nominal
assumption — high churn is uniquely expensive here, and "harvest the loss"
strategies that work for equity do nothing for VDAs.
