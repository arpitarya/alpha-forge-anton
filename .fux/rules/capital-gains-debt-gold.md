**Rule (external):** Non-equity assets follow different holding-period and rate
regimes than equity — never apply [[capital-gains-equity]] rules to them:

- **Debt mutual funds bought on/after 1 Apr 2023** ("specified mutual funds",
  s.50AA): gains are **always taxed at slab rate**, regardless of holding period.
  No LTCG treatment, no indexation. Units bought before that date keep a
  long-term regime when held past the threshold.
- **Gold** (physical, ETFs, gold funds): long-term classification applies after
  a holding-period threshold (24 months for physical post-2024 reform; listed
  gold ETFs follow the listed-security threshold). Long-term gains are taxed at
  the prevailing LTCG rate without indexation.
- **Sovereign Gold Bonds held to maturity**: redemption gains are **exempt** for
  individuals — the most tax-efficient gold route when the horizon fits.

**Source:** Income-tax Act s.50AA, s.112; reshaped by the Finance Acts of 2023
and 2024. Thresholds and rates have changed twice in two years — verify against
the prevailing Finance Act before relying on a number in code or advice.

**Applies to:** any realised-gains, rebalancing, or "which asset to sell" logic
that spans asset classes. A sale that is long-term for equity can be slab-taxed
for a debt fund bought after Apr 2023 — asset class must drive the tax bucket.
