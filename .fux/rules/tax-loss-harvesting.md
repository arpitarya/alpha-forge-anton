**Rule (external):** Capital losses offset capital gains under fixed asymmetric
rules (s.70/71/74):

- **Short-term capital loss** sets off against **both** STCG and LTCG.
- **Long-term capital loss** sets off **only** against LTCG.
- Unabsorbed losses carry forward **8 assessment years**, but only if the
  return is filed by the original due date — a late filing forfeits the
  carry-forward.
- Capital losses never offset salary or other income heads.

India has no statutory wash-sale rule — selling to book a loss and re-buying
is generally permitted — but the repurchase re-starts the holding-period clock
and resets the cost basis, and circular same-day trades may invite scrutiny.

**Source:** Income-tax Act s.70, s.71, s.74, s.80. VDA losses are excluded
entirely — see [[crypto-tax-vda]].

**Applies to:** rebalancing and year-end review advice. When trimming an
overweight class ([[capital-gains-equity]] decides the bucket), realising
losses in loss-making lots first can neutralise gains realised elsewhere in
the same year — STCL is the most flexible loss to harvest, LTCL the least.
